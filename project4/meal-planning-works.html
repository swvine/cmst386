How Meal Planning Works

Prep & Chef's meal planning system simplifies your cooking workflow, reduces decision fatigue, and helps you eat healthy meals consistently.

Start Planning View 30‑Minute Meals

Step 1: Plan Wisely

Start with our monthly meal planner. Choose meals for each week using our five core categories: Chicken, Beef, Pasta/Grain Bowls, Vegetarian/Vegan, and Soups/One-Pot Meals. Rotating categories ensures variety and easier grocery shopping.

Step 2: Prep Intentionally

Use weekly ingredient lists to organize groceries and prep ingredients ahead of time. Chop, marinate, portion, and store ingredients in labeled containers for quick access during cooking.

Step 3: Cook Efficiently

Follow step-by-step recipes designed for speed and clarity. Our 30-Minute Meals section provides additional quick options for busy days. Visual guides and tips help ensure consistent results every time.

Benefits of Meal Planning

  • Reduce stress and decision fatigue.
  • Save money by reducing food waste.
  • Cook healthier meals with minimal effort.
  • Efficiently manage time in the kitchen.
