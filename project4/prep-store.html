Prep & Store Guide

Organize your kitchen and ingredients for stress-free, efficient cooking throughout the week.

Ingredient Prep Tips

Chop vegetables, portion proteins, and measure grains in advance to save hours during the week.

Storage Best Practices

  • Use airtight containers to preserve freshness.
  • Label containers with contents and date.
  • Store vegetables and fruits separately.
  • Keep proteins refrigerated or frozen properly.

Freezer Prep

Batch cook proteins, sauces, and grains. Freeze in single-use portions for quick meal assembly.

Prep Timeline Example

Sunday (1 hour): Chop veggies, marinate proteins, portion grains.

Midweek Refresh (15 min): Check freshness, refill containers.
