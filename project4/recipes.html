Step-by-Step Recipes

Garlic Chicken Meal Prep (Beginner)

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic cloves
  • Lemon
  • Salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Mix olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Marinate chicken in mixture for 15 minutes.
  4. Bake chicken for 20 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Slice and portion into containers for meal prep.

Pesto Pasta with Veggies (Intermediate)

Ingredients

  • Pasta of choice
  • Pesto sauce
  • Zucchini
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions.
  2. Sauté zucchini and cherry tomatoes in olive oil for 5 minutes.
  3. Toss cooked pasta with veggies and pesto sauce.
  4. Serve topped with grated Parmesan cheese.
